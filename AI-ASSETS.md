> **This file is about artwork and media only.** AI assistance in *code* is a separate

## The short version

- Some of the current artwork in this repository was **generated with AI**.
- **Code is a separate matter, and AI assistance there is allowed.** See
  [AI-POLICY.md](.github/AI-POLICY.md). Nothing in this file restricts how you write code.
- We disclose this openly because hiding it is worse than the thing itself.
- **We would very much prefer real fan art**, and we will take it gladly.
- If you send us art, you give the project a **perpetual, irrevocable, worldwide,
  royalty-free licence** to use it for any purpose, and you confirm **you own it or have
  the right to licence it**. Details in the terms below.

That is the whole document. The rest is the precise wording.

## Why we say it out loud

Because the alternative is getting caught later and looking dishonest, and because people
have a right to know what they are looking at before they decide how they feel about a
project. It costs us nothing to be upfront and it costs everything to be found out.

We are not making a political statement in either direction. We needed a banner, we did not
have an artist, and we used a tool. The moment someone draws something better, the tool
output goes in the bin and the human gets the credit.

## We want your art

Specifically, we want to **replace every AI generated image in this project with art made
by a person**. Any of these are useful:

- A logo or mascot for the engine
- A banner for the repository
- A favicon and small icon set
- Skins and themes for FilianWIKI
- Loading and error page illustrations
- Pixel art, because VTuber communities are extremely good at pixel art
- Better ASCII art than the block above

Style is up to you. The only constraints are that it has to work at small sizes, it has to
be legible over the wiki's dark default theme, and it cannot be someone else's work.

### One honest complication about fan art

This project is built by fans, for a community around a VTuber. Drawing that VTuber is the
obvious thing to want to do. Be aware that an existing VTuber's character design is owned
by the talent and their agency, so a drawing of it is a derivative work of someone else's
intellectual property, and fan art of it generally exists on tolerance rather than by
right. That is fine for your personal account. It is a problem for us, because we would
then be distributing it under a licence that we cannot grant.

So, please:

- **Preferred:** original characters, mascots, abstract art, typography, patterns, and
  anything you invented. We can use these without reservation.
- **Fine with a caveat:** art in the *style* of the community, or clearly inspired by it,
  as long as it is your own drawing and not a copy of official artwork.
- **Please avoid:** direct copies or traces of official character art, official logos,
  model sheets, or anything lifted from a stream or a screenshot.

If you are not sure whether something crosses the line, ask in an issue before you spend
hours on it.

Tell us in the submission and mark the work **CC0 1.0 Universal (public domain

### 5. If you used AI

Say so, and say how much. We are not going to reject art for being AI assisted, we simply
will not label it as human made. The same honesty applies to code, and the code side of it
is governed by [AI-POLICY.md](.github/AI-POLICY.md). AI assisted submissions are labelled honestly, the same as
everything else in this file. For replacing the current AI generated art we are specifically
looking for human made work, because that is the entire point of the exercise.

### 6. What we will do with it

- Credit you in the `NOTICE` credits section and in release notes, under whatever name you

- List the asset in the inventory above with "human made" and your name or handle.
- Possibly edit it, resize it, recrop it, or recolor it to fit the layout. We will not
  claim we drew it.
- Keep using it even if you later leave the project. That is what irrevocable means, and
  it is why section 2 is written the way it is.

### 7. What we cannot promise

Because the licence is irrevocable, we generally **cannot remove artwork** from the
project's history once it has been merged and released. Git history is distributed to every
clone. If you need something removed for a serious reason, contact
vadim@vai-rice.space and we will do what we can, which may mean rewriting history if it has
not spread yet, but we cannot promise it. Please be sure before you submit.

### 8. If it turns out you did not have the rights

If a third party claims the work, you agree to help resolve it and to cover the costs that
result from your submission, to the extent the law allows. We will handle takedown requests
promptly and remove disputed assets from current builds while they are sorted out.

## How to submit

1. Open an issue using the **Artwork submission** template, or open a pull request that
   adds the file under `.github/assets/` or `assets/`.
2. Include the title you want it listed under, the name or handle you want credited, and
   whether it is human made, AI assisted, or AI generated.
3. Include the words from the template confirming you have read and agree to the terms
   above.
4. Add your entry to the inventory table in this file in the same pull request, or say in
   the issue that you would like us to do it.

Preferred formats: SVG for icons and logos, PNG with transparency for illustrations, WebP
where size matters. Include a source file if you have one, it makes future edits much
easier.
