# Discussions

Every article, template and module has a discussion at `/<page>/talk`: topics
with replies, for questions, proposals and mistakes. `/talk` lists the topics
of every page, the most recently active first.

## Topics about a change

A topic can be about one change instead of the whole page. History has a
**discuss** link on every revision, and the diff of two neighbouring
revisions has **Discuss this change**, with a link per part when the change
touched several places. The topic keeps the revision and shows its diff above
the messages, added words in green and removed ones in red. Selecting words in
the diff while writing quotes them, so the topic says exactly what it is about.

## Writing

Writing needs an account in good standing, the same right as sending a report.
Messages are Markdown: links, lists, code, emotes. `@name` tells that person.
A reply can answer one message, which is quoted above it. Authors edit and
take down their own messages.

The topic's author, patrollers and moderators mark a topic resolved or reopen
it. Moderators also take down anybody's message, lock a topic so only they can
write, and hide a topic.

## Who hears about it

- A new topic: the page's watchers, and the author of the change it is about.
- A reply: everybody who wrote in the topic.
- `@name`: that person, up to ten a message.
- A topic marked resolved: its author.

Nobody hears about their own messages. A person may send twenty messages in
ten minutes and start ten topics an hour.

## Speed

Pages render on the server and work without scripts. A message's HTML is kept
in the render cache by the hash of its text, so a long topic renders once.
Topics follow their page when it is moved.
