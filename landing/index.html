The closed alpha is recruiting

The wiki is already running for a small group. Accounts are handed out by hand until Vadim decides it is ready for everyone to sign up on their own. Until then, this is how you get in.

  • Content makers People ready to write lore, timelines and glossary entries, and to keep them current. Clippers welcome.
  • Bug finders Break things on purpose, on any device, and report what happened. Alpha means rough edges, and every one you find is one fewer at launch.
  • People with opinions Suggest improvements, actively. Now is when a change costs an evening instead of a migration.
  • Translators The interface and the articles are translatable. English and Russian come first, other languages are welcome.

What will actually be here

Written by people who watch the streams. Not generated, not padded out for ad impressions.

Lore and timeline

Streams, arcs and running storylines laid out so a newcomer can catch up in one sitting instead of scrubbing through years of VODs.

Glossary

Every emote, callback and chat term explained without killing the joke.

Clip index

Searchable and timestamped, linked back to the source rather than screenshots of screenshots.

Editing that works without JavaScript

Suggest an edit with or without an account, on any device, with scripting off. Every change is attributed, every version is kept, and nothing gets quietly rewritten.

Community and events

Watch parties, art jams and community projects, kept current by mods and editors.

Fan art, credited

Links out to the artists who made the work, plus an archive of retired designs.

Where the build actually is

The full roadmap is public, including the parts that are not done. This is the short version.

  • Done

    Decisions

    Schema, licence and stack settled and written down with reasons. The expensive thing to get wrong.

  • In progress

    Foundation and accounts

    Reader, editor, sessions, and signing in with GitHub, Discord or Telegram.

  • Next

    Search and components

    Fast search over every page, plus infoboxes the community can edit like any other page.

  • Then

    Migration from FANDOM

    Wikitext importer and an open export format, so leaving here is as easy as arriving.

Improvements are wanted, not tolerated

The whole thing is open source. Every reasonable improvement gets reviewed and, if it holds up, merged. You do not need permission to start.

  • Tell us where the design is wrong Right now this is the most valuable thing anyone can do. Editing a design document costs an afternoon. Editing a schema a thousand wikis depend on costs a year.
  • Take a good first issue Issues are labelled by area and by effort, so you can find something that fits the evening you actually have.
  • Draw something We want to replace every generated image on this project with human made art, with credit. Artists, this one is for you.
  • Write, even without code A wiki is writing. Lore, glossary entries, timelines and translations matter as much as the engine under them.

Who is building this

Vadim Khristenko, who signs his work VAI. A Rust developer who writes open source, and a Snacker, which is the part that explains why this exists at all.

The wiki engine under this site, NotAnotherWiki, is his: a Rust core, a Postgres schema designed before a line of it shipped, and a licence chosen so that no company can close it later. It is built largely in the open and mostly outside working hours, so it will take as long as it takes.
