# Welcome to FilianWIKI

The fan wiki of the Snackers, the community around Filian. Stream lore, running jokes, VOD indexes and community projects live here, written by the people who watch.

This wiki runs on the NotAnotherWiki engine: Markdown first, readable with JavaScript off, exportable in an open format. No trackers, no ads.
