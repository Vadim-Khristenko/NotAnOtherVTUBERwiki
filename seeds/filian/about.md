# About FilianWIKI

FilianWIKI is a wiki about Filian and her community, written by Snackers for Snackers: the streams, the lore, the characters, the running jokes, the clips and the fan art, all in one place you can search and link to.

## Why we are building it

Fan wikis usually live on big hosted platforms, full of ads and slow on a phone. We want the opposite: a fast wiki without ads, where every edit is on record, every picture credits its artist, and the community decides what it looks like.

## This is a fan project

- We are **not** Filian and we do not speak for her. The wiki is not an official resource and has no connection to her or her team.
- We are **not** part of any ARG. Nothing here is a clue, a puzzle or a hidden message. If something on this site looks like one, it is a joke by an editor or a mistake, and you can report it.

## How to take part

The wiki is in a **closed alpha**: accounts are made by hand while we test it. To join, write to **vadim@filian.wiki** with the subject "closed alpha" and say:

1. the username you would like,
2. your age group,
3. who you are,
4. why you want to join.

We are looking for people who write articles, people who hunt bugs, translators, and anyone with ideas.

Already in? Pick an article, press **Edit**, and cite the VOD or the post you got it from. If a page is protected, **View source** lets you suggest a change. See the [community rules](/community) first.
