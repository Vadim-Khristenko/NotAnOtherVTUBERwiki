# About FilianWIKI

FilianWIKI documents Filian's streams, lore, community projects and inside jokes. Anyone in the Snackers can propose an edit. Be kind, cite VODs, credit artists.

The wiki lives at snackers.vai-rice.space and runs on the open NotAnotherWiki engine. Everything here is written by Snackers, for Snackers.
