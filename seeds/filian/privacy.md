# Privacy policy

FilianWIKI (beta.filian.wiki) is a fan wiki made by the Snackers community. It is not affiliated with Filian, not run by her, and not part of any ARG. "We" and "the administration" below mean the wiki's administrators.

This page says what the wiki keeps about you, why, for how long, and what you can do about it. It is part of the [[terms|Terms of use]], which you accept when you sign in.

## No tracking

The wiki shows no ads and runs no analytics. Reading pages load no scripts from anyone else, and we do not record which pages you read. Page views are only counted as totals per minute, with no address or account attached, so we can see when the wiki is slow or busy.

## What we keep

**Your account.** Your username, display name and avatar. If you sign in with a password, its hash (never the password itself). If you sign in through Discord, Telegram or GitHub, the id of that account and, when the provider confirms it, its email address.

**Your edits and files.** Every edit you publish is public, with your name and its time, and stays in the page history. Files you upload are public too. Drafts you have not published are seen only by you.

**Your sign-ins.** For each browser you are signed in on, we keep its address, the name of the browser, and when it was last used. You see this list in your settings and can sign out any of them. A session's record is deleted when it ends or expires.

**Linked bots.** If you link the wiki's Telegram or Discord bot, we keep that chat or user id, its username and the language you chose for the bot. The bot sends you password reset links, warnings about new sign-ins and password changes, and the notifications you turn on. Unlinking it, in the settings or with `/unlink`, deletes the link.

**What you ask the wiki to do.** Your watchlist and notifications, the reports you send and the answers to them, and which version of these documents you accepted.

**Moderation records.** Moderation actions, such as blocks, page protection and role changes, are kept with who did them and when. Admins may keep notes about an account for moderation.

**Server logs.** Like any web server, ours records the address, time and page of each request. These logs are kept for **30 days** and then deleted. Counters that slow down password guessing and request floods hold an address for up to 15 minutes.

## Why

To run the wiki and keep it working; to let you sign in and get your account back if you lose the password; to keep the wiki safe from spam, vandalism and attacks, including blocking addresses that abuse it; and to show who wrote what, which a wiki depends on. We do not sell or rent your data, and we do not use it for advertising.

## Who else sees it

Your public edits, files, username, display name, avatar and profile are visible to everyone. Admins and moderators see what they need for moderation, such as the sessions and notes of an account. When you sign in through Discord, Telegram or GitHub, that service knows you signed in here, under its own privacy policy. Bot messages go through Telegram or Discord. Beyond that, we share data only when the law requires it.

## Cookies

One cookie keeps you signed in. Another remembers the language you picked. A third remembers that you saw the notice about a new version of these documents. A short-lived one exists for a few minutes while you sign in through another device. The editor keeps an unsaved draft in your own browser. There are no other cookies and none from anyone else.

## Deleting your account

You can delete your account in your settings, where the wiki allows it, or ask the administration to do it. Deleting an account removes **the account and the profile**: your name, profile page, avatar, sign-in methods, linked bots, drafts, watchlist and notifications. You are signed out everywhere.

**Your files stay** on the wiki. To have files you uploaded removed, write to the administration; they will deal with the request within **14 working days**.

**Your edits stay** in the public history of the pages, as commits stay in a public repository on GitHub: other people's text builds on them, and the history must stay whole. They are shown under a deleted name. Edits are not removed, unless agreed otherwise with the administration.

The administration may make exceptions in your favor, for example remove your edits entirely, at its own decision.

## Your rights

You can ask the administration what the wiki keeps about you, ask to correct it, or ask to delete it as described above. Most of it you can see and change yourself in your settings.

## Children

The [[terms|Terms of use]] say who may have an account. If you believe a child has given us data they should not have, write to the administration and we will remove it.

## Changes to this page

When this policy changes in substance, the wiki tells you the first time you sign in or visit after the change, and asks you to accept the new version when you next sign in. The administration reserves the right to change this page without telling you in advance. The version and date are below.

## Contact
