# Privacy

No trackers. No ads. No analytics. FilianWIKI runs no third-party scripts on reading pages.

What we store: your account identifier from the login provider you chose, the email address that provider shares, and the public history of your edits with timestamps. Page views are not logged per user.

Questions about your data: contact the wiki administrators.
