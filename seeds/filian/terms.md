# Terms of use

FilianWIKI (beta.filian.wiki) is a fan wiki about Filian and her community, written by its readers. It is not affiliated with Filian, not endorsed by her, and not part of any ARG. "The administration" below means the wiki's administrators.

By ticking the box when you sign in, or by using the wiki, you accept these terms and the [[privacy|Privacy policy]]. If you do not accept them, you may still read the wiki, but please do not sign in or edit.

## Who may have an account

You must be at least 13 to make an account. If the law where you live sets a higher age for agreeing to this on your own, you need a parent's or guardian's permission until you reach it. One person may have one account; a second account is allowed only with the administration's consent, for example a bot. You are responsible for what is done from your account: keep your password and sign-in methods to yourself.

Usernames that are reserved, or that pretend to be someone else, are given out by the administration only.

## What may be posted

The wiki is read by people of every age, minors included, so:

- **No sexual or pornographic content (NSFW)**, not in text, images, links or file names. No sexualized content involving minors in any form; such content is removed and reported to the authorities.
- No gore, shock content or content glorifying violence or self-harm.
- No hate, harassment, threats or bullying of anyone, on the wiki or about its people.
- **No doxxing**: no one's real name, address, photos, contacts or other private details unless they made them public themselves, and never Filian's private life beyond what she shares on stream.
- No spam, advertising or links planted for search engines.
- **No made-up facts.** Do not add information you know to be false, including fake clues, fake "leaks" and invented ARG content. Mark speculation and theories as such, and cite VODs, posts and other sources where you can.
- Respect copyright. Quote within reason and say where it comes from. Post fan art only with the artist's permission, and credit them.
- No impersonation of Filian, her team, other people or the administration, and no presenting the wiki as official.
- Nothing that breaks the law of Russia or of the United States, where most of the community reads from, or of the country you are in.

## Security and other people's data

You must not:

- try to get into anyone else's account, or to read, change or delete data that is not yours;
- try to get around blocks, rate limits, page protection or any other safeguard, including by making new accounts or switching addresses after a block;
- probe the wiki for weaknesses, or attack it, without the administration's written permission;
- flood the wiki with requests or run bots and scrapers that weigh on it, without permission;
- collect other users' data from the wiki, beyond what everyone sees on a page, for any purpose.

Found a security problem? Write to the administration privately and do not share it before it is fixed. We are grateful for that, and good faith reports are not held against you.

## Your contributions

Your words stay yours. By publishing an edit or uploading a file, you let the wiki display, keep, copy and archive it, with your authorship attached, and let others edit and build on it here. Edits stay in the public history of the page, as commits stay in a public repository; see the [[privacy|Privacy policy]] for what deleting an account removes.

Upload only what you have the right to share.

## Moderation

The administration and moderators may edit, revert, hide or remove content that breaks these terms, protect pages, and limit, block or delete accounts and block addresses, for a time or for good. A blocked address can still read the wiki but cannot write to it. If you think a decision was wrong, write to the administration and explain why.

## No warranty

The wiki is a hobby made by volunteers and is provided as is. We try to keep it right and online, but cannot promise that every article is accurate or that the wiki is always available. Use what you read here with common sense.

## Changes to these terms

When these terms change in substance, the wiki tells you the first time you sign in or visit after the change, and asks you to accept the new version when you next sign in. The administration reserves the right to change them without telling you in advance. Using the wiki after a change means you accept it.
